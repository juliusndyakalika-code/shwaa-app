# Privacy Policy — Shwaa Messages

_Last updated: 25 July 2026_

Shwaa Messages ("the app") is an SMS messaging application. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used. **In short: your messages and contacts stay on your device. The developer does not collect, store, or receive any of your personal data.**

## Information the app accesses

- **SMS messages** — The app reads, sends, receives, and displays your text messages so it can function as your SMS app. Messages are read from and written to your device's system SMS storage. They are **never** transmitted to the developer or any server.
- **Contacts** — The app reads your contacts only to display names and photos next to phone numbers. Contact data stays on your device and is never transmitted.
- **Phone** — When you tap a phone number or the call button, the app opens your device's dialer. The app does not place calls itself.

## Optional AI message feature

The app includes an optional feature that can draft a short scheduled message for you using an AI provider. This feature is **off by default** and only works if you choose to enable it and enter your own API key. When you use it, only the short text **prompt you type** (for example, "wish a happy birthday") is sent to the provider you select — **Anthropic (Claude)** at `api.anthropic.com` or **OpenAI** at `api.openai.com` — to generate the message text. Your SMS message contents and your contacts are **not** sent. If you never enable this feature, no data ever leaves your device. Data you send to these providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Data the developer collects

**None.** The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The developer has no servers and receives no data from the app.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not share or sell any personal data, because we never collect any.

## Data retention and deletion

All app data (drafts, settings, scheduled tasks) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. Your SMS messages are managed by the Android system and are not deleted by uninstalling the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
